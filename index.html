FAQ

Can we send announcements to all tenants at once?

Yes. Broadcast to your whole portfolio, a single property, specific floors, or individual tenants. These tenant communication tools let you send via push notification, email, in-app message, or all three. Schedule ahead or send now. Delivery tracking shows who received and opened each message.

How does Live Translate work?

Your team writes in their preferred language—Spanish, Portuguese, Mandarin, whatever they're most comfortable with. Work orders are automatically translated to English for tenants. It's built into work orders and team messaging, so there's no extra step. Teams document more thoroughly when they write naturally, and tenants always get clear communication.

What does the Writing Assistant do?

It takes quick, informal messages and makes them sound professional across tenant messages, work order updates, and broadcasts. Maintain a consistent tone across your property team, even when everyone's moving fast.

Can we control what content tenants see in the app?

Completely. You can build pages for your directory, amenity hours, parking rules, and move-in instructions. Edit in Visitt, and it publishes immediately. Templates keep branding consistent across properties.

How do tenants access building information and announcements?

Through the Tenant App. Broadcasts arrive as push notifications, emails, and in-app messages. Building pages are searchable within the app—tenants type what they're looking for and find it without calling your team.

Yes. The Tenant App is fully personalized with your logo, colors, and imagery so that tenants see your brand. Property admins can manage branding, pages, and settings themselves using a live editor—no code or Visitt support required.

Every tenant interaction flows into your Visitt operations platform. Requests become work orders automatically. Amenity bookings flow into billing. Recurring issues are flagged by Visitt AI for escalation. You're not managing a separate tenant system because it's all connected.

Tenants get faster responses, real-time status updates, and self-service access to building resources. They're not waiting on hold, chasing emails, or wondering if anyone saw their request. That adds up to a better experience—and better retention.
